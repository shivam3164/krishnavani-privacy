KrishnaVani Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 5, 2026

Introduction

KrishnaVani ("we", "our", or "the app") is a Hindu spiritual companion app providing Bhagavad Gita verses, Mantra Jap counter, Hindu Festival Calendar, and Temple Guide. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle information.

Information We Collect

Local Data Only: KrishnaVani stores all user data locally on your device. This includes:

  • Reading progress and bookmarks for Bhagavad Gita chapters
  • Mantra Jap counter statistics and daily streaks
  • Language preferences (English/Hindi)
  • App settings and preferences

This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device and app information
  • Ad interaction data

Google's use of advertising data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not require account creation or login
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, or microphone
  • We do not share any personal data with third parties (beyond AdMob as described above)

Children's Privacy

KrishnaVani is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require any personal data to function.

Data Security

Since all app data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security measures. We recommend using device-level security (PIN, fingerprint, etc.) to protect your data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
